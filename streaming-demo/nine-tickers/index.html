The evidence. A 180-second source excerpt from the November 17, 2026 @HUATZAITCG livestream, divided into twelve contiguous 15-second blocks. Three previously published outputs came from faster-whisper base.en, Singlish2English-small, and Coats Whisper large-v2 NSC. Text is aligned only to those blocks. The page does not run an additional speech model.

The derived layers. Group 02 compares token overlap across the three models without rewriting anything. Group 03 marks descriptive words such as “box” and “chat” and, for one block, surfaces a possible Zekrom reference suggested by cross-model phonetic retrieval. It is a hypothesis, not proof that a particular Pokémon was spoken, shown, or sold. A “Throh” false lead was also observed in the research experiment and is not promoted to an entity.

Uncertainty stays visible. No independently listened human-gold transcript, word-level timing, proven audience-event/video synchronization, card-printing identity, sale, bid, or exact price is established here. All named-entity proposals require source listening and review before an inference is accepted.
