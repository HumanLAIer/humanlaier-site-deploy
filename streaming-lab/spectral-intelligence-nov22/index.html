Enrollment, methodology and receipts

Frozen role rules use whole-recording acoustic evidence and existing audience-address cues; the thresholds were developed on this recording. Profile fitting excludes every selected evaluation block and a one-second guard. Training samples are weakly labeled mixtures, not clean isolated voices. D and E use the same total negative dictionary rank. F is a bounded repetition-similarity method inspired by REPET, not its original algorithm. G combines E and F, H blends G with the original. Unknown activity and source-clock boundaries receive conservative mask protection. No accuracy winner is claimed without independent references. No DBS-TIGER or other neural enhancement calls.
