Methodology, receipts and limitations

Frozen thresholds are from the prior development classifier. This replay uses only the verified acoustic feature values, native activity, audio-event windows and previously decoded verbatim text. It recomputes attribution and role classifications without reading human labels, prior role decisions, speaker ranking or source identity. Both likely-background and music-dominant mixed candidates can be deferred across exclusive intervals; participant, unknown and overlapping voices remain retained. This is an offline whole-recording decision, not live causal recognition; a genuine role change or slot conflation can still be missed. No audio re-encoding or new model calls. The recording informed the original thresholds, so label-blind execution is not a held-out validation.

All activity, score windows and shadow decisions are projected from saved, checksum-verified model evidence for this development recording. Source video is unchanged. Speaker slots are local hypotheses, and audio-event scores describe the mixture rather than isolated voices. Human listening labels are deliberately not used or displayed here. Single-window speech scores do not override an independently established likely-background role; overlapping and unknown voices are protected. Padding is approximated at the native 10 ms hop within source parts. Gate audition is approximate browser muting of the whole mixture, not a separated audio track. No speech has been discarded and no production gating or verified recognition-accuracy claim has been made.
