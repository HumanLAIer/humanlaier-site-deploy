Methodology, receipts and limitations

All activity, score windows and shadow decisions are projected from saved, checksum-verified model evidence for this development recording. Source video is unchanged. Speaker slots are local hypotheses, and audio-event scores describe the mixture rather than isolated voices. The human listening assessment identifies background English rap and Spanish singing in selected samples, not full-stream ground truth. Thresholds were tuned on this recording and are not independent validation. Overlap, unknown slots and contradictory speech evidence are protected. Padding is approximated at the native 10 ms hop within source parts. Gate audition is approximate browser muting of the whole mixture, not a separated audio track. No speech has been discarded and no production gating or verified recognition-accuracy claim has been made.
