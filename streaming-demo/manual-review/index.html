Default: the fixed packed A/R ASR hypotheses, comparing five frozen recognition configurations on the same text. The historical ten-lane model bake-off and eight-take acoustic diagnostics are separate optional views. Candidate ranks and aligned alternate forms come from actual experimental outputs, not fresh inference. Original source video is always played; no derivative audio substituted. Native word anchors are model estimates; absent or invalid anchors replay the source window, including any omitted gaps in packed audio.

Teal is earlier dictionary evidence; amber is a flagged review span. Candidate rank is within one frozen span, not calibrated confidence or a verified entity. Review any unhighlighted word or whole passage to identify missed errors. Unreviewed spans stay unannotated.
