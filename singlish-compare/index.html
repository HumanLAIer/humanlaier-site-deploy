03 / REPRODUCIBILITY

A real recording.
Three hypotheses.

We ranked recorded 180-second windows by observed deliberate audience activity: comments, unique accounts, gifts, likes, follows and shares. This November 17 passage ranked highest in the audited set and preserves one uninterrupted three-minute slice of the original video segment's audio.

The production baseline uses faster-whisper base.en with beam search and voice activity detection; the two challengers are Singlish2English-small and Coats Whisper large-v2 NSC. Model-loading time is reported separately from inference. Different decoding settings reflect their current implementations, so this is an operational comparison, not a perfectly normalized model contest.

Evidence limits: the November 17 capture predates sealed original-media manifest publication. The experiment binds to the actual stored video-segment bytes by R2 object version and newly computed SHA-256. Source-media/event synchronization remains provisional and does not prove causality. Outputs have not been checked against a human reference; gross provider charges are not independently reconciled.
