Evidence from the room.

The source. We selected a three-minute window for observed TCG/product and buyer-price questions rather than maximum comment volume alone. The November 17 @HUATZAITCG archive contains original video and public engagement records; exact media bytes are rehashed before inference. Media-to-chat placement derives from an FFmpeg process clock and remains provisional, not broadcast-time synchronization.

The nine-way experiment. Three pinned ASR engines—faster-whisper base.en, Singlish2English-small and Coats Whisper large-v2 NSC—each run under three different decoding conditions: control, a compact TCG glossary, and a source-bounded domain vocabulary. Every populated ticker must be backed by an actual model result tied to the same twelve source audio fingerprints. The focused glossary was selected after reviewing an initial baseline, and model-specific decoder configurations (including token budgets) differ: this is an operational comparison, not a blind normalized benchmark. A blank ticker is not simulated.

What business leaders can inspect. Whether a product name, pack/ETB distinction, shipping or price is consistently heard; which model/context settings change the words; the cost and time of processing; and separately observed buyer questions. Observed questions are not completed transactions or verified revenue. Even unanimous model agreement is not human-gold accuracy.

Release standard. ASR outputs remain uncorrected. Candidate names, bids, grades, numerical amounts and causal chat-to-speech claims are unverified until source listening and reference alignment. No independent word timestamps or grounded sales ledger are supplied here.
